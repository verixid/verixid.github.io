Built for verifiable digital provenance

VerixID computes a cryptographic fingerprint of a digital file, binds it to a timestamp, and signs the resulting registration record. The file itself never leaves your control.

Anyone can later verify that the same fingerprint was registered at that point in time — without needing the original file, the original registrar, or trust in a central authority.
